MOBILE LEGENDS HERO

GATOTKACA

Gatotkaca is a powerful Tank and Fighter hero known for his durability, crowd-control abilities, and ability to initiate team fights. He becomes stronger as he takes damage, allowing him to protect his teammates and challenge enemies during battles.

His ultimate, Avatar of Guardian, allows him to leap into the air and land at a selected location, knocking airborne nearby enemies into the air. This powerful ability makes him effective at starting team fights, disrupting enemy formations, and creating opportunities for his teammates.
