MOBILE LEGENDS HERO

AAMON

Aamon is a powerful Assassin hero known for his camouflage abilities, magic damage, and high mobility. As the Duke of Shards, he uses magical shards to attack enemies while temporarily concealing himself. He specializes in surprising opponents and eliminating vulnerable targets.

His ultimate, Endless Shards, allows him to throw all his available shards toward a selected enemy. The shards return to the target after a short delay, dealing magic damage that increases as the enemy loses health. This makes him effective at finishing off weakened opponents.
