MOBILE LEGENDS HERO

ALDOUS

Aldous is a powerful Fighter hero known for his devastating physical damage and ability to become stronger as the match progresses. As the Soul Contractor, he collects Soul Steal stacks to increase the damage of his enhanced basic attacks. His abilities make him especially dangerous during the late game.

His ultimate, Contract: Chase Fate, allows him to reveal the locations of all enemy heroes across the battlefield. He can then select an enemy and dash toward them, dealing physical damage and knocking the target back. This makes him effective at chasing enemies and targeting important opponents.
