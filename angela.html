MOBILE LEGENDS HERO

ANGELA

Angela is a lovely Support hero known for her healing abilities and incredible ability to protect her teammates. She helps her allies survive battles, provides healing, and controls enemies with her powerful abilities.

Her ultimate, Heartguard, allows her to attach herself to an allied hero anywhere on the battlefield. She provides a shield to protect her teammate and can use her abilities while attached, helping her team during important team fights.
