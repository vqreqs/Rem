MOBILE LEGENDS HERO

CICI

Cici is a Fighter hero known for her mobility, continuous physical damage, and ability to chase enemies. As the Buoyant Performer, she uses her magical Yo-Yo to attack opponents, move around the battlefield, and increase her movement speed through her passive ability.

Her ultimate, Curtain Call, allows her to link two enemy heroes together with her Yo-Yo. The linked enemies are slowed and pulled toward each other, allowing Cici to attack both targets using her Yo-Yo Blitz. This makes her effective at damaging multiple opponents during team fights.
