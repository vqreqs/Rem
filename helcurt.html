MOBILE LEGENDS HERO

HELCURT

Helcurt is a deadly Assassin hero known for his high burst damage, mobility, and ability to surprise enemies. He specializes in hunting down important targets and eliminating vulnerable opponents. His skills allow him to quickly approach enemies and deal heavy damage before they can react.

His ultimate, Dark Night Falls, allows him to cover the battlefield in darkness, reducing the enemies' vision and disrupting their coordination. He also gains increased movement speed and attack speed, allowing him to quickly chase down enemies and create opportunities for his team.
