MOBILE LEGENDS HERO

ALPHA

Alpha is a powerful Fighter hero known for his durability, sustained damage, and ability to control enemies. Together with his companion Beta, he uses advanced technology to attack opponents, deal continuous damage, and recover health during battles.

His ultimate, Spear of Alpha, allows Beta to dive toward a selected location before Alpha follows with a powerful leap. He knocks enemies airborne, deals physical damage, and allows Beta to attack the area repeatedly. This makes him effective at initiating fights and disrupting enemy formations.
