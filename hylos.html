MOBILE LEGENDS HERO

HYLOS

Hylos is a durable Tank hero known for his incredible health, defensive abilities, and powerful crowd-control skills. As a guardian of the Sacred Forest, he uses his strength to protect his teammates, stun enemies, and control the battlefield. He can also use his own health to maintain his abilities.

His ultimate, Glorious Pathway, creates a pathway that increases the movement speed of allied heroes and restores their health while they move along it. Enemies moving against the pathway have their movement speed reduced. This ability helps his team chase enemies, escape dangerous situations, and initiate team fights.
