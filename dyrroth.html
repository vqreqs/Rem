MOBILE LEGENDS HERO

DYRROTH

Dyrroth is a powerful Fighter hero known for his high burst damage, mobility, and ability to weaken enemy defenses. As the Prince of the Abyss, he uses his dark powers to attack opponents, recover health, and overwhelm enemies with powerful physical attacks.

His ultimate, Abysm Strike, allows him to unleash a destructive attack in a selected direction. It deals heavy physical damage based partly on the target's lost HP and slows enemies hit. This makes him effective at finishing weakened opponents during team fights.
